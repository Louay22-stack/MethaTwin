# Sources

All sources were consulted on 2026-11-20. "Verification" says how each fact was checked:

- **Primary, read**: the page or PDF was opened and the figure was read there.
- **Primary, via search**: an official page, but the figure comes from a search-engine summary. Re-check it before quoting it in the final report.
- **Secondary**: press or consultancy coverage of a primary document that was not opened.

## Facts I could not verify

- The exact 2025 number of biomethane plants in Europe. Figures in the press differ depending on the dataset (statistical report vs biomethane map), so none is used.
- Total national RNG production capacity in Canada. No authoritative figure was found.
- Whether Quebec's proposed delay of the 10 % RNG target to 2032 was adopted. This is reported in the press but not confirmed, so it is not used as fact.
- Whether a QSDsan ADM1 implementation exists. It was not confirmed, so PyADM1 and the BSM2 reference (L2, L3) are used instead.
