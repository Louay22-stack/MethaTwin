# MethaTwin

**See your plant work before it exists.** MethaTwin is an ML-accelerated digital twin of a ~200 Nm³/h farm biomethane plant, from the digester to the gas grid. Try a design and get a plain answer in seconds: is the biology healthy, is the gas grid-ready, does it pay?

- ADM1 biology (BSM2 form, matched to the international benchmark) and DWSIM water-scrubbing upgrading
- ML surrogates of the full simulation, about 1,000× faster
- Techno-economics anchored on the French regulator's (CRE) cost survey, Monte Carlo risk over 2,000 futures
- Built from public data and open-source tools only

Code, methods and report: [github.com/Louay22-stack/MethaTwin](https://github.com/Louay22-stack/MethaTwin)

*Model predictions from public data, not investment advice.*
